# NovanceModding

**Welcome to NovanceModding** – a collective of developers focused on creating high-quality, open-source Minecraft mods that push the game beyond its limits.

> **A ChitterSync-owned initiative**  
NovanceModding is proudly backed by [ChitterSync](https://chittersync.com), a platform empowering creators, gamers, and developers to share, collaborate, and innovate freely.

## Who We Are

NovanceModding is built by a passionate team of creators dedicated to enhancing the Minecraft experience through innovative, performance-friendly, and community-focused mods. Whether it's improving core gameplay, adding unique features, or building utility frameworks, we’re here to level up the sandbox.

## Our Philosophy

- **Open Source Always** – Transparency and collaboration power everything we do.
- **Performance First** – Mods shouldn’t lag your game. Ours are built with optimization in mind.
- **Player-Focused** – Feedback, fun, and functionality drive our development process.
- **Creative Freedom** – Build the way you want. Play the way you want.
